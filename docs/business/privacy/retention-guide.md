# Retention guide

Not legal advice, review locally before use.

| What                             | Default                                          | Range | Where to change it                             | How it is deleted                              |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Video and photos                 | Never stored                                     | —     | —                                              | Frames stay in memory only                     |
| Anonymous table visits           | 90 days                                          | 7–730 | Settings → Data kept                           | Rules job, every 15 s                          |
| Staff time in work areas         | 60 days                                          | 7–365 | Privacy & consent → What we keep               | Rules job, every 15 s                          |
| Queue readings                   | Same as staff time                               | —     | —                                              | Rules job                                      |
| Face template                    | Until withdrawal or removal                      | —     | Privacy & consent → Withdraw consent or Remove | At once                                        |
| Staff time history of one worker | Until withdrawal or removal, or the period above | —     | Privacy & consent → Withdraw consent or Remove | At once                                        |
| Till orders                      | Kept as sales records                            | —     | Not deleted by DineMate                        | Unlinked from the worker when they are removed |
| Audit of report views            | Kept with the shop's audit log                   | —     | —                                              | [__ your policy]                               |

## Choosing your periods

- Keep staff time only as long as you really use it, for example one rota cycle plus a month: [__ days].
- Shorter is safer. Weekly and monthly summaries in your own records can replace raw times.
- Write the chosen periods into the staff notice so workers see the same numbers as the dashboard.

## When someone leaves

1. Print their sheet if they ask for a copy.
2. Press **Remove** in Privacy & consent. Their template and time history are deleted; their till orders stay as anonymous sales.
3. Destroy or archive the paper consent form according to [__ your policy].
