## Why we use it

We use DineMate to see how busy the shop is and to plan staffing: whether someone is at the counter, how long queues get, and how much each till code serves. It is not used to [__ list anything you will not use it for, for example: discipline for short breaks, or decide pay on its own].

## What the cameras do

- Cameras at [__ locations] watch the floor, the counter and the kitchen. Video is processed on a computer in the shop and is **not recorded**.
- Customers are counted at tables and in the queue. They are **never identified**.
- If you agree, the system recognises you **only while you stand in a marked work area** (counter, kitchen or floor), either by your face or by a printed badge you wear. Outside those areas you are not recognised.
